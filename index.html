How to play
  • Tap a unit to select it. Double-tap selects every unit of that type on screen.
  • Drag to look around. With units selected a drag box-selects instead — or hold still, then drag, to box-select any time. A building in hand never takes the drag away.
  • Tap the ground to move, a resource to gather, an enemy to attack.
  • Two fingers always pan, and pinch to zoom.
  • Villagers gather food, wood, gold and stone, and drop off at the nearest place that takes it.
  • Build houses for population, a barracks for soldiers, then go win.
  • Each arm has its own building. Barracks for infantry, Archery Range for archers and skirmishers, Stable for cavalry. You cannot afford all three at once — scout what they built, then answer it.
  • Spearmen beat cavalry, skirmishers beat archers, archers beat infantry, cavalry runs archers down. Tap any unit to read what it is strong against.
  • Reached the Castle Age? A Siege Workshop builds rams to break walls and mangonels to break formations — a mangonel hurts everything near where the rock lands, including your own men, and cannot hit anything standing right next to it. Keep it behind the line and spread out.
  • A Blacksmith upgrades every soldier you already own, and a Monastery trains monks who heal them. Monks never fight.
  • Rock cannot be walked through. Neither can water. Use both — a wall only has to cover the gap between two outcrops.
  • Select the Town Center and advance the age — it unlocks the gathering and blacksmith upgrades, and every building of yours gets tougher.
  • Select a Mill, Lumber Camp, Mining Camp or Barracks to research upgrades there. They apply to everything you already own.
  • Long walk home? Put a Lumber Camp by the trees or a Mining Camp by the gold — villagers switch to it on their own.
  • Tap Jobs in the bar at the bottom to set what share of your villagers works each resource. Switch it on and the game keeps them matching it.
  • Placing a building stays armed: tap out a whole row of houses in one go, and your builders work through the queue by themselves. The dock button on the left says Done while you are placing.
  • Walls are drawn, not placed. Arm a Palisade or Stone Wall and drag: one straight swipe is one straight wall, and a swipe that turns gets a corner. Drag to the edge of the screen and the map follows. Put a Gate straight into a finished wall — it replaces the segment and gives its cost back.
  • Put a building somewhere you regret? Select it and tap Demolish twice.
