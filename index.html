    How to play
    • Tap a unit to select it. Double-tap selects every unit of that type on screen.
    • Drag to look around. With units selected, drag to box-select — or hold, then drag, any time.
    • Tap the ground to move, a resource to gather, an enemy to attack.
    • Two fingers always pan, and pinch to zoom.
    • Villagers gather food, wood, gold and stone, and drop off at the nearest place that takes it.
    • Build houses for population, a barracks for soldiers, then go win.
    • Select the Town Center and advance the age — it unlocks the gathering and blacksmith upgrades, and every building of yours gets tougher.
    • Select a Mill, Lumber Camp, Mining Camp or Barracks to research upgrades there. They apply to everything you already own.
    • Long walk home? Put a Lumber Camp by the trees or a Mining Camp by the gold — villagers switch to it on their own.
    • Tap Jobs in the bar at the bottom to set what share of your villagers works each resource. Switch it on and the game keeps them matching it.
    • Placing a building stays armed: tap out a whole row of houses in one go, and your builders work through the queue by themselves.
    • Put a building somewhere you regret? Select it and tap Demolish twice.
